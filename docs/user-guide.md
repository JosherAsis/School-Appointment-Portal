## Getting Started

### Registration

1. Navigate to the registration page by clicking "Register" in the navigation bar
2. Fill in your personal information:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (minimum 6 characters)
   - Student ID
   - Department (optional)
   - Year Level (optional)
3. Click "Register" to create your account
4. You will be automatically logged in and redirected to the student dashboard

### Login

1. Navigate to the login page by clicking "Login" in the navigation bar
2. Enter your email address and password
3. Click "Login" to access your account
4. You will be redirected to your dashboard based on your role (student or admin)

### Forgot Password

If you forget your password, you can reset it:

1. Click "Forgot password?" on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Click "Send Reset Link"
4. Check your email for a password reset link
5. Click the link in the email
6. Enter a new password
7. Click "Reset Password"
8. You can now log in with your new password

## Student Guide

### Student Dashboard

The student dashboard provides quick access to all features:

- Book a new appointment
- View your existing appointments
- Update your profile information

### Booking an Appointment

1. From the student dashboard, click "Book Now" or navigate to "Book Appointment" in the navigation bar
2. Select a date for your appointment
3. Choose an available time slot
4. Enter the reason for your appointment
5. Click "Book Appointment"
6. You will receive a confirmation email with the appointment details

### Managing Your Appointments

1. From the student dashboard, click "View Appointments" or navigate to "My Appointments" in the navigation bar
2. View a list of all your appointments with their status
3. To cancel an appointment:
   - Find the appointment you want to cancel
   - Click the "Cancel" button
   - Confirm the cancellation
   - You will receive a cancellation confirmation email
4. To reschedule an appointment:
   - Cancel the existing appointment
   - Book a new appointment

### Updating Your Profile

1. From the student dashboard, click "Edit Profile"
2. Update your information as needed
3. Click "Save Changes"

## Administrator Guide

### Admin Dashboard

The admin dashboard provides comprehensive tools for managing the appointment system:

- View appointment statistics
- Manage pending appointments
- Configure time slots
- Access the student directory

### Managing Appointments

1. From the admin dashboard, click "Manage" under "Manage Appointments"
2. View a list of all appointments
3. Filter appointments by status, date, or student
4. To update an appointment status:
   - Click on the appointment
   - Change the status (approve, reject, mark as completed)
   - Add admin notes if needed
   - Click "Update"
   - The student will receive an email notification about the status change

### Configuring Time Slots

1. From the admin dashboard, click "Configure" under "Manage Time Slots"
2. View a list of all existing time slots
3. To add a new time slot:
   - Click "Add Time Slot"
   - Select the day of the week
   - Enter start and end times
   - Set the maximum number of appointments for this slot
   - Click "Save"
4. To edit a time slot:
   - Click the "Edit" button next to the time slot
   - Update the information
   - Click "Save"
5. To delete a time slot:
   - Click the "Delete" button next to the time slot
   - Confirm the deletion

### Managing Students

1. From the admin dashboard, click "View Directory" under "Student Directory"
2. View a list of all registered students
3. Search for students by name, email, or student ID
4. Click on a student to view their details and appointment history

## Troubleshooting

### Login Issues

- **Forgot Password**: Use the "Forgot password?" link on the login page
- **Account Locked**: Contact the system administrator
- **Invalid Credentials**: Double-check your email and password

### Appointment Issues

- **Cannot Book Appointment**: Ensure you are selecting a future date and an available time slot
- **Cannot Cancel Appointment**: You can only cancel appointments with "pending" status
- **No Available Time Slots**: Contact the administrator to check time slot availability

### Email Notifications

- **Not Receiving Emails**: Check your spam folder
- **Wrong Email Address**: Update your email in your profile settings

### Technical Issues

If you encounter any technical issues:

1. Try refreshing the page
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Try using a different browser
4. Contact technical support if the issue persists
